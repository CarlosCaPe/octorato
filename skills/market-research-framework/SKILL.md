# Market Research Framework — Consulting-Grade Analysis

## Purpose
Systematic framework for conducting comprehensive market research at McKinsey/Bain/BCG level. Contains 12 structured analysis modules that cover the full spectrum from market sizing to market entry strategy.

## The 12 Modules

### Module 1: Market Sizing & TAM Analysis
**Persona:** Senior market analyst (McKinsey level)

**Deliverables:**
1. **Top-down approach** — Global market segment → narrowed to target
2. **Bottom-up approach** — Unit economics × potential customers
3. **TAM / SAM / SOM breakdown** with dollar figures
4. **5-year CAGR projections** with growth drivers
5. **Key assumptions** behind each estimate (sourced)
6. **Benchmarks** — Compare with 3 analyst reports or research firms

**Format:** Investor-ready market sizing slide with clear methodology.

**Required inputs:** `[INDUSTRY/PRODUCT]`, `[TARGET CUSTOMER]`, `[GEOGRAPHY]`

---

### Module 2: Competitive Landscape Analysis
**Persona:** Senior strategy consultant (Bain & Company level)

**Deliverables:**
1. **Direct competitors** — Top 10 ranked by market share, revenue, funding
2. **Indirect competitors** — 5 adjacent players who could enter
3. **Per-competitor analysis:** pricing model, key features, target audience, strengths, weaknesses, recent strategic moves
4. **Positioning map** — Price vs. value matrix
5. **Competitive moats** — What makes each player defensible
6. **White space analysis** — Gaps no competitor covers
7. **Threat assessment** — Low / Medium / High per competitor

**Format:** Structured competitive intelligence report with comparison tables.

**Required inputs:** `[INDUSTRY]`, `[YOUR BUSINESS + POSITIONING]`

---

### Module 3: Customer Profiling & Segmentation
**Persona:** World-class consumer research expert

**Deliverables:** 4 detailed personas, each with:
1. **Demographics** — Age, income, education, location, job title
2. **Psychographics** — Values, beliefs, lifestyle, personality traits
3. **Pain points** — Top 5 daily frustrations
4. **Goals & aspirations** — What success looks like for them
5. **Buying behavior** — Discovery → evaluation → purchase journey
6. **Media consumption** — Where they spend time (online + offline)
7. **Objections** — Top 3 reasons they'd say no
8. **Trigger events** — What moment makes them actively seek a solution
9. **Willingness to pay** — Price sensitivity by segment
10. **Segment sizing** — % of total market + prioritization matrix

**Required inputs:** `[PRODUCT/SERVICE]`, `[INDUSTRY]`

---

### Module 4: Industry Trend Analysis
**Persona:** Senior analyst (Goldman Sachs Research level)

**Deliverables:**
1. **Macro trends** — 5 global forces (economic, regulatory, tech, social, environmental)
2. **Micro trends** — 7 emerging patterns from last 12 months
3. **Technology disruptions** — Game-changing new technologies
4. **Regulatory shifts** — Upcoming legislation or policy changes
5. **Consumer behavior shifts** — How buyer preferences are evolving
6. **Investment signals** — Where smart money flows (VC, M&A, IPO)
7. **Timeline mapping** — Each trend tagged: short (0-1yr), mid (1-3yr), long (3-5yr)
8. **"So what" analysis** — What each trend means for the business
9. **Impact ratings** — 1-10 per trend

**Format:** Trend intelligence report.

**Required inputs:** `[INDUSTRY]`, `[YOUR BUSINESS + MARKET]`

---

### Module 5: SWOT + Porter's Five Forces
**Persona:** Strategy professor (Harvard Business School level)

**SWOT deliverables:**
- 7 Strengths with evidence
- 7 Weaknesses with honest assessment
- 7 Opportunities (external)
- 7 Threats (external)
- **Cross-analysis:** SO strategy (strengths × opportunities) + WT risks (weaknesses × threats)

**Porter's Five Forces deliverables:**
1. Supplier power — Key suppliers + bargaining leverage
2. Buyer power — Customer bargaining strength
3. Competitive rivalry — Intensity + drivers
4. Threat of substitutes — Alternatives beyond direct competitors
5. Threat of new entrants — Entry barriers assessment
- **Each force rated 1-10** with overall industry attractiveness score

**Required inputs:** `[COMPANY]`, `[PRODUCT]`, `[INDUSTRY]`, `[STAGE]`

---

### Module 6: Pricing Strategy Analysis
**Persona:** Pricing strategist (Simon-Kucher level)

**Deliverables:**
1. **Competitor pricing map** — Feature tiers × price points
2. **Value-based pricing** — Customer willingness-to-pay analysis
3. **Cost-plus analysis** — Unit cost + margin targets
4. **Price elasticity** — Sensitivity curves by segment
5. **Pricing models evaluation** — Subscription, freemium, usage-based, one-time, tiered
6. **Recommended pricing architecture** — Good/Better/Best tiers with feature allocation
7. **Psychological pricing tactics** — Anchoring, decoy, charm pricing
8. **Revenue impact modeling** — 3 scenarios (aggressive, base, conservative)

**Required inputs:** `[PRODUCT]`, `[COMPETITORS]`, `[COST STRUCTURE]`, `[TARGET SEGMENT]`

---

### Module 7: Go-to-Market Strategy
**Persona:** Chief Strategy Officer (20+ product launches)

**Deliverables:**
1. **Launch phases** — Pre-launch (60d), Launch (week 1), Post-launch (90d)
2. **Channel strategy** — Top 7 acquisition channels ranked by expected ROI
3. **Messaging framework** — Core value prop + 3 supporting messages + proof points
4. **Content strategy** — What to create for each funnel stage
5. **Partnership opportunities** — 5 strategic partners for growth acceleration
6. **Budget allocation** — How to distribute `[BUDGET]` across channels
7. **KPI framework** — 10 metrics with target benchmarks
8. **Risk mitigation** — Top 5 launch risks + 3 contingency plans
9. **Quick wins** — 3 tactics for traction in first 14 days

**Format:** Actionable GTM playbook with timelines and owners.

**Required inputs:** `[PRODUCT]`, `[MARKET]`, `[BUDGET]`, `[TIMELINE]`

---

### Module 8: Customer Journey Mapping
**Persona:** CX strategist (tier-1 consulting firm)

**Stages mapped:** Awareness → Consideration → Decision → Onboarding → Engagement → Loyalty → Churn

**Per stage:**
1. Customer actions, thoughts, and emotions
2. Touchpoints (digital + physical)
3. Pain points and friction moments
4. Opportunities to delight
5. Key metrics to track
6. Recommended tools/tactics to optimize

**Additional:** Emotional curve visualization (text-described).

**Required inputs:** `[PRODUCT]`, `[CUSTOMER TYPE]`, `[CURRENT CONVERSION RATE]`

---

### Module 9: Financial Modeling & Unit Economics
**Persona:** VP Finance (high-growth startup)

**Unit economics:**
- CAC by channel
- LTV calculation with detailed assumptions
- LTV:CAC ratio + payback period
- Gross margin per unit/customer
- Contribution margin analysis

**3-year projection:**
- Revenue model (monthly Y1, quarterly Y2-Y3)
- Cost structure (fixed vs. variable)
- Break-even analysis (when + at what volume)
- Cash flow projection with burn rate
- Sensitivity analysis (best / base / worst)

**Format:** Financial model summary with tables and formulas.

**Required inputs:** `[BUSINESS MODEL]`, `[CURRENT REVENUE]`, `[COSTS]`, `[GROWTH RATE]`

---

### Module 10: Risk Assessment & Scenario Planning
**Persona:** Risk management partner (Deloitte level)

**Risk categories:**
1. Market risks (demand shifts, competition, pricing pressure)
2. Operational risks (supply chain, talent, tech failures)
3. Financial risks (cash flow, FX, funding gaps)
4. Regulatory risks (compliance, policy changes, legal exposure)
5. Reputational risks (PR crisis, customer backlash, data breach)

**Per risk:**
- Probability (1-5) × Impact (1-5) = Risk score
- Early warning signals
- Mitigation strategy
- Contingency plan

**Scenario planning:** Best case, base case, worst case with financial impact.

**Required inputs:** `[BUSINESS/PROJECT]`, `[TIMELINE]`, `[KEY DEPENDENCIES]`

---

### Module 11: Market Entry & Expansion Strategy
**Persona:** Global expansion strategist (30+ market entries)

**Market attractiveness assessment:**
- Market size + growth rate
- Competitive intensity
- Regulatory environment
- Customer accessibility
- Infrastructure readiness
- Each factor rated 1-10 with weighted total

**Entry mode evaluation:**
- Direct entry (build from scratch)
- Alliance / Joint venture
- Acquisition
- Licensing / Franchise
- Digital-first entry
- Pros, cons, cost, timeline for each

**Required inputs:** `[BUSINESS]`, `[TARGET MARKET/GEOGRAPHY/SEGMENT]`

---

### Module 12: Executive Summary & Synthesis
**Persona:** McKinsey engagement manager

**Deliverables:**
1. **One-page executive summary** — SCQA format (Situation, Complication, Question, Answer)
2. **Key findings matrix** — Top 5 insights from each module used
3. **Strategic recommendations** — Prioritized 1-2-3 with rationale
4. **Action plan** — 30-60-90 day roadmap
5. **Decision log** — What decisions need to be made and when


## Quality Standards

### Sources
- Always cite sources: Statista, CB Insights, PitchBook, Crunchbase, SEC filings, analyst reports
- Distinguish between data (sourced) and estimates (modeled)
- State confidence level for each major claim: High (multiple sources) / Medium (single source) / Low (estimate)

### Format Rules
- Tables over paragraphs for comparisons
- Numbers over adjectives ("$2.4B TAM" not "large market")
- Always include methodology transparency
- Provide both optimistic and conservative estimates

### Anti-Patterns
- Never fabricate market data — say "estimated" or "not available" 
- Never present single-source claims as consensus
- Never skip the "So what?" — every data point needs business implications
- Never mix TAM with SAM — keep the funnel clear
